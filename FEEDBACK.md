# Feedback

[Português (Brasil)](FEEDBACK.pt-BR.md)

This page is for anyone who used helplito.org and wants to report a problem or suggest something. You do not need to know how the site is built.

If you are Lito, his family, his team or an institution mentioned on the site, use [CLAIMS.md](CLAIMS.md) instead. If you want to change the code yourself, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Two addresses, two purposes

Feedback about the website goes to helplito@brunosilva.io or to a GitHub issue in this repository.

Help Lito no longer forwards scientific or clinical information to Lito's team. The site is now a memorial, and the form that prepared those messages was removed after his death.

Please do not send suggestions for unverified treatments to any address.

## What is useful to report

- A page that does not load, or shows an error.
- Text that overflows, overlaps or is cut off on your screen. Say which device and browser you used.
- A link that goes to the wrong place or is broken.
- A wrong, awkward or inconsistent translation in either language.
- A screen reader, keyboard or contrast problem.
- Something that is unclear, or information you looked for and could not find.
- A medical or scientific statement that you believe is inaccurate or outdated. Include the source.
- A clinical trial whose status on the site no longer matches the official registry.
- A prion disease organization or research group that is missing. Include its official website.

## How to report

### By email

Write to helplito@brunosilva.io. One problem per message is easier to handle, but several is fine. Screenshots help. Tell us the page address, your device, your browser and your language.

### On GitHub

Open an issue at https://github.com/brunos3d/helplito.org/issues and pick the template that fits: bug report, content or translation correction, or general feedback. Issues are public; do not include personal information about yourself or anyone else.

## What to expect

Every message is read by the person who runs the site. Bugs that break a page or block the contact form are fixed first. Translation and wording fixes are usually quick. Content corrections take longer because the source is checked before anything changes.

This is a volunteer project with one person behind it, so replies can take a few days. If you reported something through an issue, you will see the fix land as a commit that references it.

We do not add features that collect money, track visitors, or publish user-submitted content, so feedback asking for those will be declined with a short explanation.

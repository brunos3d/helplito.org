# Claims, corrections, removals and ownership

[Português (Brasil)](CLAIMS.pt-BR.md)

This page is for people the site is about, or people who have a legal or personal interest in its content: Lito Sousa, his family, his official team, Aviões e Músicas, and any person, researcher, physician, organization or institution named on helplito.org.

Requests from these people take priority over everything else in this project. You do not need a GitHub account, you do not need to open an issue, and you do not need to explain yourself in public.

## How to reach us

Write to helplito@brunosilva.io. The address is answered by Bruno Silva, the person responsible for the website. Write in Portuguese or English.

Do not use ajudalito@avioesemusicas.com for requests about this website. That address belongs to Lito's official team, and Help Lito does not read it.

If you prefer a public record, you can open an issue using the "Correction or removal request" template, but an email is faster and keeps personal details out of a public repository.

## What you can ask for

### Correction

Anything on the site that is wrong, outdated or misleading. This includes medical or scientific statements, trial status, organization details, dates in the timeline, and anything written about Lito or his family. Tell us what is wrong and, when possible, what the correct information is and where it comes from. If you are the person concerned, your word is the source.

### Removal

Any text, photograph, name, link or page. You do not have to give a reason. If a request concerns a photograph of you or your family, it is honored as soon as it is read.

The site currently shows four photographs of Lito, one of which also shows his wife. They were taken from material the family shared publicly. If the family wants any or all of them removed or replaced, that happens without discussion.

### Removal of an organization or institution

Organizations are listed because they work on prion disease, not because they agreed to be listed. If your organization does not want to appear on the site, or wants its description, link or contact information changed, write to us. The entry is removed or changed at your request.

### Ownership, administration and handover

Help Lito was created to help, not to control the narrative around Lito's case. If Lito, his family, his official team, Aviões e Músicas or another appropriately authorized party wants to discuss:

- taking over the helplito.org domain,
- taking over this repository and the hosting,
- co-administering the site,
- changing what the site says or how it is run, or
- taking the site down entirely,

that conversation is welcome, and it starts with an email. Nothing transfers automatically; the point is that the door is open and the answer is not "no" by default.

A handover would include the domain, the hosting project, this repository, the initiative email address and any credentials, with no conditions attached other than confirming that the person asking is who they say they are.

### Legal concerns

Privacy, image rights, defamation, copyright or any other legal matter: write to the same address. Include enough detail to identify the content and the basis of the concern. The content is taken down while the matter is reviewed if there is any doubt.

## How we verify who you are

For corrections of public facts, we usually do not need to verify anything; we check the source and fix it.

For removals and ownership requests, we need to know the request really comes from the person or organization it claims to. Usually that means one of:

- a reply from an address at a domain the organization publicly controls,
- a message through a channel Lito's team has published (for example, from the official email), or
- a short exchange to confirm the details.

We keep this proportionate. Nobody has to send identity documents to get a photograph taken down.

## What happens next

1. You get a reply confirming the email was received. Usually within a day or two; this is a volunteer project with one person behind it.
2. For removals: the content comes down first, then we reply. For corrections: we check and fix, then reply with what changed.
3. The change is committed to this public repository with a commit message that describes it in neutral terms. Your email, your name (unless you are already named on the site) and the content of your message are not published.
4. If we cannot do what you ask, or cannot do it fully, we say so and explain why.

## What we will not do

- We will not argue with the family or the official team about what the site should say about Lito.
- We will not publish your request or your correspondence.
- We will not add a "disputed" label or a note about a request instead of making the change. If content is removed, it is removed.
- We will not forward anything you send us to anyone else unless you ask for it.

## A note on fraudulent campaigns

Lito's official team has warned about fraudulent fundraising campaigns using his name. Help Lito does not accept donations and never will. If you find a campaign or website that claims to be Help Lito, or that uses this site's name, design or content to ask for money, tell us at helplito@brunosilva.io and report it to the official team as well.

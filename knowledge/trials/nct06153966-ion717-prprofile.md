# ION717 / PrProfile / NCT06153966

Registry evidence rechecked 2026-08-25 BRT (UTC-03:00); sponsor-attributed and
case-specific status updated 2026-08-25.
The record contains a current internal recruitment conflict, so no single status
sentence should be quoted without its source field and date.

## Current status by evidence layer

- **Registry status:** ClinicalTrials.gov version 30 lists the study as
  `Active, not recruiting`, removes site statuses and the central contact, and
  says expanded access is unavailable. The detailed description still contains
  conflicting recruitment language.
- **Sponsor operational status:** Ionis publicly stated, in a company response
  reproduced by UOL on August 25, that the study is no longer recruiting new
  participants. No current site referral or screening path is confirmed.
- **Case-specific status:** the family reports that Ionis declined Lito's
  inclusion. Ionis confirms that the individual matter is being handled
  privately, but does not publish the case decision. No current ION717 path for
  Lito has been identified.
- **Expanded access:** Ionis's general policy lists no ION717 program and says
  an unlisted medicine or condition has no currently available Ionis program.
  The August 25 company response separately says ION717 is not currently
  available through Ionis expanded-access programs.
- **Regulatory status:** no primary source reviewed here says FDA must
  personally approve ordinary participation in this authorized trial.
  Individual expanded access is a different process, but sponsor supply is the
  present upstream blocker.

## Sponsor

ClinicalTrials.gov and CTIS identify Ionis Pharmaceuticals, Inc. as sponsor. The
current Ionis pipeline identifies ION717 as an investigational RNA-targeted
medicine designed to inhibit production of prion protein and places it in Phase
1/2a. This mechanism and development status do not establish efficacy.

## Study design

The current registry describes a screening period of up to six weeks, a 30-week
treatment period, a 142-week open-label extension and a 32-week post-treatment
period.

The record contains three regimens:

- Regimen 1: multiple doses of ION717 and placebo during a blinded 30-week
  treatment period, followed by ION717 in the open-label extension;
- Regimen 2: the same high-level blinded ION717 and placebo structure, followed
  by ION717 in the open-label extension;
- Regimen 3: ION717 during the 30-week treatment period and the open-label
  extension, with no placebo intervention listed for this arm.

The detailed description says Regimens 1 and 2 are fully enrolled and Regimen 3
is open-label.

The overall design module still says randomized, parallel and double-masked
because those attributes apply to the study across regimens. They do not make
the separately described Regimen 3 placebo-controlled.

## Recruitment state

Current version 30 shows `Active, not recruiting`. All 13 locations remain
listed, but every location-specific status value and the central contact were
removed.

The current detailed description was not updated with those fields. It still
says the listed sites are actively recruiting eligible participants for
Regimen 3. This is an unresolved conflict inside the current official record.

The Ionis neurology pipeline agrees with `Active, not recruiting`, but some
design numbers on that page reflect an older registry state, including
approximately 56 participants and a 70-week extension. ClinicalTrials.gov
version 30 reports 85 actual participants and a 142-week extension.

## Eligibility published by the registry

The current record lists key criteria, including confirmed probable or definite
prion disease, early-stage disease at screening, age 18 or older, ability and
willingness to meet study requirements and an adult caregiver able and willing
to support participation. Additional medical and investigator-assessment
criteria apply.

This summary does not assess any individual against those criteria.

## What the sources do not establish

The reviewed sources do not establish:

- a participant count broken down by Regimen 1, 2 and 3;
- a currently open Regimen 3 site after version 30;
- that NYU's `Open` label is current rather than stale or locally scoped;
- immediate slot or screening capacity;
- individual eligibility, acceptance or enrollment;
- access to ION717 outside the study;
- safety or efficacy of ION717.

The sponsor-attributed response establishes present operational closure and
current ION717 expanded-access unavailability. It does not establish permanent
closure or rule out a future cohort or program.

### C-006: Registry metadata versus sponsor operational status

ClinicalTrials.gov's structured field says `Active, not recruiting`, while its
detailed description still says Regimen 3 sites actively recruit. Ionis says
the study is no longer recruiting new participants. This is operationally
resolved for current sponsor availability but remains an unresolved registry
metadata conflict.

## Sponsor description

ClinicalTrials.gov and CTIS identify Ionis Pharmaceuticals, Inc. as sponsor. The
current Ionis pipeline identifies ION717 as an investigational RNA-targeted
medicine intended to inhibit production of prion protein and places it in Phase
1/2a. These mechanism and development statements do not establish efficacy.

## Study design and regimens

The current detailed description gives a screening period of up to six weeks, a
30-week treatment period, a 142-week open-label extension and a 32-week
post-treatment period.

The record describes three regimens:

- Regimen 1: multiple doses of ION717 and placebo during a blinded 30-week
  treatment period, followed by ION717 in the open-label extension;
- Regimen 2: the same high-level blinded ION717 and placebo structure, followed
  by ION717 in the open-label extension; and
- Regimen 3: ION717 during the 30-week treatment period and the open-label
  extension, with no placebo intervention listed for this arm.

The detailed description says Regimens 1 and 2 are fully enrolled and Regimen 3
is open-label.

The structured design module describes the trial overall as randomized,
parallel and double-masked, with participants and investigators masked. The arm
module separately describes Regimens 1 and 2 as blinded ION717-plus-placebo
regimens and Regimen 3 as ION717-only and open-label. Overall design metadata
and regimen-specific design are different scopes; the overall metadata does not
make Regimen 3 placebo-controlled.

## Current recruitment state

Version 30 shows `Active, not recruiting`. All 13 locations remain listed, but
every location-specific status value and the central study contact were removed.
The absence of a current site value must not be converted into either
`Recruiting` or `Active, not recruiting` at site level.

The current detailed description was not reconciled with those changes. It
still says the listed sites are actively recruiting eligible participants for
Regimen 3. This is an unresolved conflict inside the current official record.

The Ionis neurology page agrees with the current overall state. Its ION717 entry
cites a March 5, 2026 access date and still reports approximately 56 participants
and a 70-week extension. ClinicalTrials.gov version 30 instead reports 85 actual
participants and a 142-week extension.

## Eligibility published by the registry

The current record lists key criteria including confirmed probable or definite
prion disease, early-stage disease at screening, age 18 or older, ability and
willingness to meet study requirements, and an adult caregiver able and willing
to support participation. Additional medical and investigator-assessment
criteria apply.

This summary does not assess any individual against those criteria.

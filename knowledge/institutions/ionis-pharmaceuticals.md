# Ionis Pharmaceuticals

## Relationship to ION717

ClinicalTrials.gov identifies Ionis Pharmaceuticals, Inc. as sponsor and
responsible party for PrProfile, NCT06153966. Ionis's current pipeline places
ION717 in Phase 1/2a for prion disease.

The mutable recruitment statements published through Ionis-controlled sources
must be read with their timestamps. The current registry version was posted
August 19, 2026 and reports `Active, not recruiting`; the Ionis neurology page
also displays `Active, not recruiting`, but its citation identifies a registry
snapshot accessed March 5, 2026. Agreement between those labels does not make
the older webpage a current operational statement.

## F-ION-004 — Published expanded-access availability

**Finding:** Ionis has a corporate expanded-access policy, but no current
ION717 expanded access program was found in the company's published list.

**Status:** verified for the published list observed at the timestamp above.

The policy says that when a condition or investigational medicine is not listed
on the page, there is no currently available Ionis expanded access program for
it. The only program listed during this verification was zilganersen for
Alexander disease in the United States. ION717 and prion disease were not
listed.

The ION717 ClinicalTrials.gov record independently sets its expanded-access
field to unavailable. This is supporting trial-record evidence, not a substitute
for the sponsor's policy page.

## Policy scope

Ionis publishes criteria it may consider, including disease severity, absence
of a satisfactory alternative, inability to join a trial, available clinical
evidence, active development, effects on clinical trials and regulatory review,
product supply, and the existence of a regulatory mechanism in the relevant
country or region.

The policy also provides a channel through which physicians may ask Ionis about
investigational medicines. The existence of that channel is not evidence of a
program, product supply, approval, or an affirmative decision for any person.

## Interpretation limits

This finding is temporal and narrow:

- a corporate policy is not a product-specific program;
- a regulatory expanded-access pathway is not sponsor agreement to supply;
- absence from the current list does not establish permanent impossibility;
- the record does not address an unpublicized, future, emergency, or
  case-specific sponsor decision.

Recheck the live policy and trial record before reusing this finding.

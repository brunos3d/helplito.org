# NeuroNEXT

## Institutional role

NeuroNEXT is the Network for Excellence in Neuroscience Clinical Trials. Its
official site says the network is funded by NIH's National Institute of
Neurological Disorders and Stroke (NINDS) and includes clinical sites, a
Clinical Coordinating Center and a Data Coordinating Center.

## Formal relationship to PRiSM

NeuroNEXT lists `NN112 PRiSM` as an active project, identifies Eric Vallabh
Minikel, PhD, as Protocol Principal Investigator and lists the same five
clinical sites that appear in ClinicalTrials.gov.

Broad says PRiSM is supported by NeuroNEXT, which provides funding and
infrastructure for the trial. Broad specifically names the trial sites, the
Clinical Coordinating Center at Massachusetts General Hospital's Neurology
Department, and the data and statistics center at the University of Iowa.

Broad's funding statement says NINDS funds the trial through NeuroNEXT under
grant `3OT2NS138339-01S2`. ClinicalTrials.gov separately names NINDS as a
collaborator and links NIH identifier `OT2NS138339`.

## Coordinating centers

- NeuroNEXT identifies its Clinical Coordinating Center at the Neurological
  Clinical Research Institute at Massachusetts General Hospital. The network
  describes project management, regulatory coordination, site management,
  protocol-design support and related network-wide functions.
- NeuroNEXT identifies its Data Coordinating Center at the University of Iowa
  Clinical Trials Statistical & Data Management Center. The network describes
  protocol coordination, statistics, data management and monitoring functions.

The network pages describe general functions. Broad's PRiSM announcement is the
source that explicitly associates both centers with this trial.

## Clinical sites for NN112

- University Hospitals/Case Western Reserve;
- Columbia University Medical Center;
- Massachusetts General Hospital;
- Mayo Clinic Rochester; and
- Vanderbilt University.

ClinicalTrials.gov uses slightly different labels for Mayo and the Cleveland
site. This note preserves the official labels from each source.

## Interpretation limits

The documented relationships do not establish:

- ownership or control among the institutions;
- a patient-facing referral or re-routing policy;
- real-time screening or dose capacity;
- individual eligibility or enrollment; or
- that network-wide coordinating-center functions are performed identically in
  every detail for NN112.

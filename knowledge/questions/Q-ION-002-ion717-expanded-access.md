# Q-ION-002 — Is ION717 currently available through expanded access?

## Current answer

No current ION717 expanded access program was found in the official public
sources reviewed on August 24, 2026.

Ionis's policy says that if a condition or investigational medicine is not
listed on its page, there is no currently available Ionis program for it. The
page listed zilganersen for Alexander disease and did not list ION717 or prion
disease. The current NCT06153966 record also reports that expanded access is not
available.

## What this establishes

The company's published program list did not include ION717 at the observation
time. This supports a time-bounded statement about public program availability.

## What this does not establish

- permanent impossibility of access outside a trial;
- that Ionis would deny or approve a future or case-specific request;
- that FDA or ANVISA authorization has been requested or granted;
- that the existence of a regulatory pathway creates product supply;
- any person's eligibility for a trial or access pathway.

## What could change the answer

A current Ionis listing or statement, a product-specific expanded-access
protocol or IND, or an official registry update showing ION717 availability.
Because the policy and product-development state can change, this answer must be
reverified before publication or operational use.

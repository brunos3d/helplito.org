# Q-ION-002 — Is ION717 currently available through expanded access?

## Current answer

Ionis says ION717 is not currently available through its expanded-access
programs. This product-specific operational statement is the current answer.

Ionis's policy says that if a condition or investigational medicine is not
listed on its page, there is no currently available Ionis program for it. The
page listed zilganersen for Alexander disease and did not list ION717 or prion
disease. The current NCT06153966 record also reports that expanded access is not
available.

Separately, the family reports a negative case-specific answer for Lito. That
private case result remains family-attributed.

## What this establishes

The sponsor's product-specific statement, the general program list and the
registry all point to current unavailability. Their scopes remain distinct.

## What this does not establish

- permanent impossibility of access outside a trial;
- that Ionis will maintain the same position permanently;
- that FDA or ANVISA authorization has been requested or granted;
- that the existence of a regulatory pathway creates product supply;
- any person's eligibility for a trial or access pathway.
- worldwide or permanent unavailability based on Lito's case-specific answer.

## What could change the answer

A new Ionis listing or statement, a product-specific expanded-access protocol
or IND, an official registry update, or another sponsor-authorized pathway.
Because the policy and product-development state can change, this answer must be
reverified before publication or operational use.

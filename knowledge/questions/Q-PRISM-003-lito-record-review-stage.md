# Q-PRISM-003 — What stage follows the reported request for Lito's records?

## Question

Does the reported request for medical records represent an informal
pre-screening review, a formal study-screening step or another process?

## Current evidence

Folha reports, based on the family's account, that Broad requested records for
evaluation and that the family awaited a meeting. The ClinicalTrials.gov record
describes eligibility criteria but does not identify any case or expose an
individual screening workflow.

## What would resolve this

A dated official family update and/or Broad or study-team statement that names
the review stage without disclosing private medical information.

## Interpretation limits

A request for records is not equivalent to eligibility, formal screening,
enrollment, acceptance, allocation or slot availability.

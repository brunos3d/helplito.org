# Q-007: PRiSM interventional path for Lito

Are any PRiSM sites or cohorts currently able and willing to evaluate Lito for
the interventional arm, or is the control/non-intervention pathway the only
operational option presently available to him?

The registry lists the study and five sites as `Recruiting`, but does not
publish real-time capacity by arm or cohort. Broad confirms active contact and
evaluation of options. The family reports that the concrete path discussed so
far is non-interventional. Neither statement establishes an Arm 2 review,
rejection or future ineligibility.

A dated answer identifying an expedited Arm 2 review route or the exact
protocol, dose-cohort, capacity or eligibility blocker, plus a protocol-backed
explanation of whether Arm 1 affects later Arm 2 eligibility or priority, would
resolve the question.

# Q-007: PRiSM interventional path for Lito

Are any PRiSM sites or cohorts currently able and willing to evaluate Lito for
the interventional arm, or is the control/non-intervention pathway the only
operational option presently available to him?

The registry lists the study and five sites as `Recruiting`, but does not
publish real-time capacity by arm or cohort. The family reports that the current
Broad path presented to Lito is non-interventional. This does not establish an
Arm 2 rejection or future ineligibility.

A dated answer from authorized PRiSM operations identifying an active Arm 2
review route, plus a protocol-backed explanation of whether Arm 1 affects later
Arm 2 eligibility or priority, would resolve the question.

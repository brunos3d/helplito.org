# Q-ION-001 — What does Ionis mean by "not accepting new participants" while Regimen 3 is registered as recruiting?

## Question

What exact operational scope is intended by secondary reports that attribute
“not accepting new participants” to Ionis, while the current registry still
contains prose saying listed sites actively recruit for open-label Regimen 3?

## Current evidence

The conflict has narrowed since the earlier August snapshot:

- ClinicalTrials.gov version 30 is now `Active, not recruiting` overall;
- the same current version retains the Regimen 3 recruitment sentence;
- all site states and the central contact were removed in version 30;
- Ionis's neurology page says `Active, not recruiting`; and
- NYU Langone still says `Open` without a visible update date.

A recent secondary report says Ionis is not accepting new participants, but the
review did not locate a public, current Ionis statement that defines what the
reported phrase means. The secondary report therefore does not replace the
registry or resolve its internal conflict.

## Possibilities to investigate

The following remain hypotheses:

- no new referrals are accepted globally;
- a temporary operational pause is in effect;
- Regimen 3 is full but its registry prose is stale;
- some sites are still screening candidates already referred;
- a country or site remains operational while new global enrollment is closed;
- the press wording simplified a narrower statement; or
- recruitment changed after the underlying interview or contact.

## What would resolve this

A dated primary statement from Ionis that defines Regimen 3 availability by
global, country and site scope, and a corresponding update to the registry's
detailed description and site fields.

## Interpretation limits

`Active, not recruiting`, “not accepting,” an open local intake form, continued
screening and continued dosing are different states. This question makes no
claim about a particular person's eligibility or access.

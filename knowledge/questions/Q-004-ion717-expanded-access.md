# Q-004: ION717 expanded access

Does Ionis currently make ION717 available through an expanded access program?

## Current answer

No evidence was found in the current official sources that an ION717 expanded
access program is available.

The Ionis policy page says that if a specific program is not listed for a
condition or investigational medicine, Ionis has no currently available program
for it and clinical-trial participation is the only access route. The current
page lists one program, for zilganersen in Alexander disease. It does not list
ION717 or prion disease.

The current ClinicalTrials.gov ION717 record independently says expanded access
is not available.

## Policy criteria

Ionis says it considers criteria including:

- serious or immediately life-threatening disease;
- no comparable or satisfactory alternative therapy;
- inability to qualify for an ongoing clinical trial;
- sufficient clinical evidence for a favorable potential benefit-risk judgment;
- active development for the requested indication;
- no negative impact on trials or regulatory review;
- adequate product supply;
- an applicable regulatory mechanism in the country or region.

Meeting policy criteria does not create a program or require Ionis to provide an
investigational medicine.

## Falsification attempt

The current Ionis policy's list of available programs and the ION717 registry
expanded-access field were checked. ION717 was absent from the list and the
registry field was false. No sponsor announcement of an ION717 program was
located in the current Ionis materials reviewed.

## What could change the answer

A new Ionis listing, sponsor announcement or official registry expanded-access
record for ION717. Because the policy says it may be revised, this answer must be
rechecked rather than treated as permanent.

## Interpretation limits

This answer concerns current public evidence of a program. It does not predict a
future sponsor decision and is not individual medical advice.

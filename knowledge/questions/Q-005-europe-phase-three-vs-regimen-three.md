# Q-005: European Phase 3 versus Regimen 3

What source and terminology produced the public description of a "European
Phase 3 without placebo" study?

## Current evidence

ION717 PrProfile is Phase 1/2a. It includes European sites and an open-label
Regimen 3 without a placebo intervention listed for that arm. CTIS confirms the
same ION717-CS2 trial in France, Germany, Italy and Spain and classifies it as an
integrated Phase I and Phase II trial. CTIS says recruitment has ended in all
four countries.

A current ClinicalTrials.gov search also identifies a different Phase 3 CJD
study, NCT07482085, sponsored by Xuanwu Hospital in Beijing. That study is in
China, not Europe, and is explicitly randomized, double-blind and
placebo-controlled. It does not match the "European" or "without placebo"
description.

## Hypothesis

The wording may have conflated `Regimen 3` with `Phase 3` and the European sites
with a European Phase III trial. The source evidence makes that explanation
plausible but does not prove what the speaker meant.

## Falsification attempt

- ClinicalTrials.gov was searched for prion-disease studies and Phase 3 records.
- The ION717 ClinicalTrials.gov and CTIS records were checked for phase, regimen,
  country and placebo design.
- The Ionis pipeline was checked for a Phase 3 ION717 program.
- The separate Phase 3 CJD result was checked for geography and masking.

No official source reviewed in this round identifies ION717 Regimen 3 as Phase
III. The separate Phase 3 study does not match the reported geography or
placebo-free design.

## What would resolve this question

The original dated statement and its cited study identifier, sponsor, protocol
or institution. Without that provenance, the intended trial remains unresolved.

## Interpretation limits

Do not publicly correct the speaker based only on this hypothesis. The confirmed
conclusion is narrower: ION717 Regimen 3 is not a Phase III designation.

# Q-005: European Phase 3 versus Regimen 3

What source and terminology produced the public description of a "European
Phase 3 without placebo" study?

## Current evidence

ION717 PrProfile is Phase 1/2a. It includes European sites and an open-label
Regimen 3 without a placebo intervention listed for that arm. CTIS confirms the
same ION717-CS2 trial in France, Germany, Italy and Spain and classifies it as an
integrated Phase I and Phase II trial. CTIS says recruitment has ended in all
four countries.

A current ClinicalTrials.gov search also identifies a different Phase 3 CJD
study, NCT07482085, sponsored by Xuanwu Hospital in Beijing. That study is in
China, not Europe, and is explicitly randomized, double-blind and
placebo-controlled. It does not match the "European" or "without placebo"
description.

## Current result: likely terminology confusion, not confirmed

The wording likely conflated `Regimen 3` with `Phase 3` and the European sites
with a European Phase III trial. The evidence makes that explanation plausible,
but the original statement and its source identifier remain unavailable, so the
interpretation is not confirmed.

## Falsification attempt

- ClinicalTrials.gov was searched again for prion-disease and CJD studies, then
  the returned conditions and Phase 3 records were inspected. The only matching
  Phase 3 CJD record found was NCT07482085 in China; it is randomized,
  double-masked and placebo-controlled.
- CTIS medical-condition searches for `prion`, `Creutzfeldt-Jakob` and `CJD`
  found no matching European Phase III prion-treatment study. ION717-CS2 was the
  prion result and is classified as integrated Phase I/II.
- The current ION717 ClinicalTrials.gov and CTIS records were checked for phase,
  regimen, country and placebo design.
- The current Ionis pipeline, last updated June 24, 2026, and the Ionis neurology
  page were checked for a Phase 3 ION717 program; none was found.
- WHO ICTRP was attempted, but its public portal returned an error for the
  reproducible filtered search. It is not counted as negative evidence.

No official source reviewed in this round identifies ION717 Regimen 3 as Phase
III. The separate Phase 3 study does not match the reported geography or
placebo-free design. This search is a falsification attempt, not proof that no
unregistered or newly registered study can exist.

## What would resolve this question

The original dated statement and its cited study identifier, sponsor, protocol
or institution. Without that provenance, the intended trial remains unresolved.

## Interpretation limits

Do not publicly correct the speaker based only on this hypothesis. The confirmed
conclusion is narrower: ION717 Regimen 3 is not a Phase III designation.

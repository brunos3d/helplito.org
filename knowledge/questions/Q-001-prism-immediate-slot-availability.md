# Q-001: PRiSM immediate slot availability

Can candidates be referred or re-routed between PRiSM sites when one site has no
immediate capacity?

## Current evidence

ClinicalTrials.gov and NeuroNEXT list five clinical sites. The registry exposes
central and site-specific study contacts. Neither reviewed source publishes
real-time capacity or a cross-site referral policy.

## Falsification attempt

The NeuroNEXT project, network, coordinating-center and contact pages were
checked for a routing commitment or patient-facing referral procedure. No such
commitment was located.

## What would resolve this question

An official NN112 policy, sponsor statement or network procedure that expressly
describes whether and how candidate referrals can move between sites.

## Interpretation limits

The existence of central and site contacts does not imply that routing is
available or required. This question is not a request for mass outreach.

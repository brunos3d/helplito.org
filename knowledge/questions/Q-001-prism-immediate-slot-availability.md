# Q-001: PRiSM interventional-arm availability and routing

Can candidates be referred or re-routed between PRiSM sites when one site or arm
has no immediate capacity, and is any site currently able to evaluate Lito for
Arm 2?

## Current evidence

ClinicalTrials.gov and NeuroNEXT list five clinical sites. The registry exposes
central and site-specific study contacts. Neither reviewed source publishes
real-time capacity or a cross-site referral policy. The family reports that the
current Broad path presented to Lito is non-interventional; this does not
establish Arm 2 availability at the other sites. Broad separately confirms
active contact and evaluation of options, but not an Arm 2 assessment.

## Falsification attempt

The NeuroNEXT project, network, coordinating-center and contact pages were
checked for a routing commitment or patient-facing referral procedure. No such
commitment was located.

## What would resolve this question

A dated operational answer identifying an expedited Arm 2 route or the exact
blocker, plus whether and how candidate referrals can move between sites.

## Interpretation limits

The existence of central and site contacts does not imply that routing is
available or required. This question is not a request for mass outreach.

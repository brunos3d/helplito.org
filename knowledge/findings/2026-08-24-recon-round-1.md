# Recon round 1

Verification window: approximately 2026-08-24 15:54 to 16:05 BRT
(UTC-03:00).

This report rechecks an earlier set of leads. It is an index, not a patient
assessment, treatment recommendation or statement that a slot or investigational
medicine is available. Source state may change after the verification window.

## Executive summary

The current ClinicalTrials.gov record confirms the main PRiSM study facts and
lists five locations as recruiting. It does not establish immediate capacity at
any location. The record identifies PRiSM as NN112, the Broad Institute as lead
sponsor and NINDS as a collaborator. NeuroNEXT identifies the same project and
sites, with network coordinating centers at Massachusetts General Hospital and
the University of Iowa.

The ION717 record changed materially during August 2026. Version 29, posted on
August 5, showed `Recruiting` and site-level statuses. Version 30, posted on
August 19, shows `Active, not recruiting`, actual enrollment of 85 and no
site-level status values or central contact. However, the unchanged detailed
description still says the listed sites are actively recruiting for open-label
Regimen 3. NYU Langone still labels its local page `Open`. CTIS says recruitment
has ended in France, Germany, Italy and Spain. These official representations
must remain separate and temporally attributed.

Ionis publishes an expanded access policy, but its current list of available
programs contains zilganersen only. The ION717 registry record also says expanded
access is not available. A general policy is not evidence of an ION717 program.

## Confirmed

### F-001: PRiSM registry identity and design

Result: confirmed. Confidence: high.

ClinicalTrials.gov identifies NCT07444580 as PRiSM, an interventional Phase 1,
first-in-human, open-label, single-ascending-dose study of intrathecal
PrP-targeting siRNA in adults with symptomatic prion disease. The lead sponsor
is the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard. Estimated total enrollment is 30.

The Broad announcement says the Phase 1 trial will enroll 15 patients, while the
registry estimates 30 total participants and describes both an observational
arm and an investigational-drug arm. The sources do not explicitly reconcile
the two numbers, so the total-versus-treated-arm interpretation remains a limit,
not a resolved fact.

### F-002: Five PRiSM locations are listed as recruiting

Result: confirmed within registry scope. Confidence: high.

The current record lists these locations with `Recruiting` status:

- Massachusetts General Hospital;
- Mayo Clinic;
- Columbia University Medical Center;
- University Hospitals Cleveland Medical Center;
- Vanderbilt University Medical Center.

This confirms registry status only. It does not establish a slot, screening
appointment, operational capacity, eligibility or acceptance at any site.

### F-003: Vanderbilt and Amanda Peltier roles

Result: confirmed. Confidence: high.

The registry identifies Vanderbilt University Medical Center as the Nashville
study location and Amanda Peltier, MD, as that location's principal
investigator. Separate people are identified with the registry role `Contact`.
An official Vanderbilt source also documents Amanda Peltier as a Vanderbilt
NeuroNEXT co-investigator. These roles must not be collapsed into a contact list.

### F-004: PRiSM network and coordinating-center relationships

Result: confirmed with interpretation limits. Confidence: high.

- ClinicalTrials.gov uses sponsor protocol identifier `NN112` for PRiSM.
- NeuroNEXT names the project `NN112 PRiSM` and lists the same five clinical
  sites.
- ClinicalTrials.gov names NINDS as a collaborator and links NIH award
  `OT2NS138339`.
- Broad says the trial is funded by NINDS through NeuroNEXT under grant
  `3OT2NS138339-01S2`.
- NeuroNEXT places its Clinical Coordinating Center at the Neurological Clinical
  Research Institute at Massachusetts General Hospital.
- NeuroNEXT places its Data Coordinating Center at the University of Iowa.

The sources support funding and operational network relationships. They do not
establish that the network or sponsor must transfer a referral between sites.

### F-005: PRiSM functional threshold

Result: confirmed. Confidence: high.

The current registry lists, as a key inclusion criterion, no more than moderate
functional impairment quantified by an `MRC-PDRS score >=15`. The registry says
additional criteria are evaluated at screening. It does not state in the public
record a more precise measurement time for this criterion.

No comparison to any individual's publicly discussed score was made. The
earlier hypothesis that a separate `16/20` statement referred to MRC-PDRS lacks
sufficient primary evidence for this repository and is excluded from the
knowledge record.

### F-006: Current ION717 overall state

Result: confirmed as changed. Confidence: high.

ClinicalTrials.gov version 30, submitted August 17 and posted August 19, shows:

- overall status `Active, not recruiting`;
- status verified August 2026;
- actual enrollment of 85;
- no location-level status values;
- no central study contact in the current record;
- no expanded access.

Version 29, submitted August 3 and posted August 5, instead showed overall
status `Recruiting`, estimated enrollment of 76, a central contact and nine
locations as recruiting. The August 19 version therefore refutes the lead that
the current overall registry state is `Recruiting`.

### F-007: ION717 regimen descriptions

Result: partially verified because the current record conflicts with itself.
Confidence: high.

The current record says Regimens 1 and 2 are fully enrolled and used ION717 and
placebo in the double-blind treatment period. It describes Regimen 3 as
open-label, with ION717 and no placebo arm. The unchanged detailed description
also says listed sites are actively recruiting for Regimen 3. That last sentence
conflicts with the current overall status and removal of every location status.

### F-010: Ionis expanded access state

Result: confirmed. Confidence: high.

Ionis has a public expanded access policy and lists criteria it considers. The
current page says that when no program is listed for a condition or medicine,
Ionis has no currently available program for it. The only program listed on the
page is for zilganersen in Alexander disease. ION717 is not listed, and the
current ION717 registry record says `hasExpandedAccess: false`.

No evidence was found in the current policy that an ION717 expanded access
program is available.

### F-011: United States single-patient expanded access roles

Result: confirmed as a general regulatory pathway. Confidence: high.

FDA describes individual-patient and emergency individual-patient expanded
access. A licensed physician oversees care and reporting. The company must be
willing and able to provide the investigational product and normally supplies a
letter of authorization or supporting product information. FDA decides whether
the request may proceed, and an IRB has a review role. In an emergency, FDA may
authorize by rapid communication before the written submission, with follow-up
deadlines. This is general process information, not individual medical advice.

### F-012: Brazil assistance programs and forms

Result: confirmed as a general regulatory pathway. Confidence: high.

ANVISA currently identifies three assistance programs under RDC 38/2013:
expanded access, compassionate use and post-study supply. Its current page says
the sponsor or sponsor representative organization, called an ORP, applies to
ANVISA. The forms page, modified March 3, 2026, lists specific forms for expanded
access, compassionate use, patient presentation, sponsor responsibility,
physician responsibility, import quantity and patient information/adhesion.

ANVISA authorization is not evidence that a manufacturer has agreed to supply a
specific medicine. The pathway depends on sponsor or manufacturer cooperation.

### F-013: Conditional consular capability in Nashville

Result: confirmed, conditional and intelligence-only. Confidence: high.

The Consulate-General of Brazil in Atlanta lists an honorary consular office in
Nashville, Tennessee. The official page says honorary consuls may assist
families of Brazilians hospitalized with serious illnesses and provide general
information, but cannot perform consular services. This becomes operationally
relevant only if a Brazilian is in the United States. It does not establish any
clinical-trial or treatment role.

### F-015: Public data model does not represent location state

Result: confirmed as a documentation gap. Confidence: high.

The current `ClinicalTrial` type stores `locations: string[]` and one overall
`status`. ClinicalTrials.gov history demonstrates that location state can differ
from overall state and can disappear in a later version. Any type change should
be discussed and implemented separately after maintainer review.

## Refuted

### F-008: ION717 is currently recruiting overall

The hypothesis is refuted by the current ClinicalTrials.gov version. It was
correct for version 29 posted August 5, 2026, but version 30 posted August 19
changed the overall state to `Active, not recruiting` and removed site statuses.

### F-009: ION717 Regimen 3 is a European Phase III trial

The equivalence is refuted. ClinicalTrials.gov and CTIS both classify
ION717-CS2 as Phase 1/2a, not Phase III. `Regimen 3` is an arm label within that
trial, not a phase designation. CTIS currently says recruitment ended in all
four participating EU countries: France, Germany, Italy and Spain.

Whether a public reference to a "European Phase 3 without placebo" was intended
to describe Regimen 3 remains unresolved. No correction of the speaker is
supported by this research.

### F-016: A current PRiSM `Recruiting` label guarantees availability

The implication is refuted. Neither ClinicalTrials.gov nor NeuroNEXT publishes
real-time slot capacity or guarantees immediate evaluation at a listed site.
Registry status and immediate availability are different claims.

## Conflicting evidence

### C-001: ION717 current registry description versus registry status

- ClinicalTrials.gov version 30 overall status: `Active, not recruiting`.
- Version 30 location statuses: absent for all 13 listed locations.
- Version 30 detailed description: still says the listed sites are actively
  recruiting eligible participants for open-label Regimen 3.

Classification: unresolved internal conflict in the current official record.

### C-002: ION717 ClinicalTrials.gov versus NYU Langone

- ClinicalTrials.gov version 30: overall `Active, not recruiting`, with no
  current NYU location status.
- NYU Langone study page: `Recruitment Status: Open` when checked during the
  verification window. The page exposes no visible last-updated date.

Classification: unresolved. The NYU page may be current, locally scoped or
stale. Its visible content does not distinguish these possibilities.

### C-003: ION717 United States page versus European registry

- The current ClinicalTrials.gov description says listed sites actively recruit
  for Regimen 3, despite the overall status conflict.
- CTIS says recruitment ended in France, Germany, Italy and Spain, with country
  recruitment end dates in 2024.

Classification: partially explained by registry scope and timing, but the
ClinicalTrials.gov description remains misleading without historical context.

### C-004: PRiSM enrollment count

- ClinicalTrials.gov estimates 30 total participants across observational and
  investigational arms.
- Broad's April 22, 2026 announcement says the Phase 1 trial will enroll 15
  patients.

Classification: unresolved scope difference. It may describe treated patients
versus total study enrollment, but neither cited source states that explicitly.

## Open questions

- Q-001: Can candidates be referred or re-routed between PRiSM sites when one
  site has no immediate capacity?
- Q-002: At what exact screening visit and under what administration rules is
  the PRiSM MRC-PDRS threshold measured?
- Q-003: Is ION717 Regimen 3 closed to new enrollment everywhere, or does any
  institution remain open under a local status not yet reconciled with version
  30?
- Q-004: Is any individual-patient or programmatic ION717 expanded access route
  available outside the publicly listed Ionis programs?
- Q-005: What source and terminology produced the public "European Phase 3
  without placebo" description?

## Public-site candidates

- Refresh PRiSM verification from the current registry while retaining the
  warning that recruitment does not mean immediate availability.
- Consider a future, separate data-model proposal for optional site-level status
  and verification date.
- Do not add ION717 as `Recruiting` based on an older registry version or the NYU
  page alone.
- If ION717 is added later, expose the current overall status, verification date
  and official-source conflict clearly.

## No-publication / intelligence-only findings

- Individual trial contacts are available at the official registries. They are
  not reproduced here and must not become a mailing list.
- The Vanderbilt principal-investigator and network roles are useful for an
  institutional map, not for personal outreach.
- Nashville consular capability is conditional travel support, not evidence of
  treatment access.
- The unverified Ministry of Health lead is excluded pending a primary source.
- The `16/20` scale hypothesis is excluded because the available evidence does
  not establish that it is the PRiSM MRC-PDRS instrument.

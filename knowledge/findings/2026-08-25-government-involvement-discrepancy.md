# Government involvement: subsequent action partially resolves the discrepancy

Updated August 25, 2026.

Earlier on August 25, the family reported that no effective government
assistance producing access was visible to it. Later that day, Health Minister
Alexandre Padilha reported that the Ministry was in contact with the family and
international researchers and had formalized a request for Lito's inclusion in
the initial study process. Itamaraty confirmed mobilizing Brazilian embassies;
reporting identifies the United States, France and Germany.

## C-005 lifecycle

```text
T1: family reports no concrete government result
  -> T2: Ministry and Itamaraty confirm specific outreach
  -> current: outreach confirmed; eligibility and access unresolved
```

Classification: partially resolved through subsequent action. Neither the
earlier family observation nor the later government action establishes an
interventional assessment, acceptance, enrollment or treatment.

The remaining public-safe questions are whether the outreach reached the
relevant PRiSM decision-makers, what response was received and whether any
diplomatic contact identified a genuinely new study path. Government and
diplomatic channels can support consideration but cannot determine eligibility,
reserve a slot or compel administration of the intervention.

Reporting that screening remains subject to FDA does not establish that FDA
personally approves ordinary PRiSM enrollment. That precise regulatory claim
still requires primary-source verification.

# Lito PRiSM screening progress

## Reported development

Folha de S.Paulo reported on August 23, 2026, based on the family's account,
that the Broad Institute sent an email requesting Lito's medical records for an
evaluation. The report says the family was awaiting scheduling of a meeting and
describes Nashville as a possible destination if he advanced to an in-person
visit and an attempt at study inclusion.

## Source

This is a secondary, case-specific report. The article was published August 23,
2026 at 20:19 BRT and shows an update at 21:46 BRT. The reviewed source does not
include a Broad statement or reproduce the reported email.

## What this establishes

**F-LITO-001:** Broad has reportedly requested medical records for evaluation.
That is evidence of reported case review progress beyond a general public
appeal.

## What this does not establish

- eligibility;
- completion or even formal start of registry-defined screening;
- enrollment;
- trial acceptance;
- treatment allocation;
- immediate availability of a slot;
- an invitation to travel; or
- confirmation that Nashville will be the study site.

## Confirmation needed

An official family update and/or a dated statement from Broad or the authorized
study team defining the stage of review and any next step.

## Privacy boundary

No medical-record content, medical detail from the record request or private
correspondence is copied into this repository.

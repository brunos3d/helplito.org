# Case-specific trial-access update

Updated August 25, 2026.

## Evidence scope

The case-specific statements below come from Mila Seidl and the family's public
update through Lito's official channel. This is primary evidence for what the
family reports it was told, but the underlying institutional communications
were not independently obtained. They are therefore not presented as confirmed
statements from Ionis or Broad.

## F-017: ION717 / PrProfile case-specific status

- **Public registry:** ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06153966 as `Active, not
  recruiting` and says expanded access is unavailable. Its detailed description
  still contains conflicting recruitment language documented in the existing
  ION717 records.
- **Case-specific status:** the family reports that Ionis declined Lito's
  inclusion, said the studies were not accepting new participants, and said
  compassionate-use access was not available to him at this time.

This reported answer means ION717 is not a current access path for Lito. It does
not establish that the medicine is unavailable to everyone worldwide or
permanently.

## F-018: PRiSM case-specific path

- **Public registry:** ClinicalTrials.gov lists PRiSM and all five sites as
  `Recruiting`. The study includes a non-intervention observational arm and an
  investigational-drug arm. A registry label does not publish real-time capacity
  by arm or cohort.
- **Case-specific status:** the family reports that the current option presented
  through Broad would be on the control/non-intervention side and would not
  provide the experimental intervention, with practical relocation implications
  and no guaranteed later treatment path.

This is not evidence that PRiSM rejected Lito, that he is ineligible, or that
non-intervention participation is the only path that could ever exist. The
actionable question is whether another PRiSM site or cohort can currently
evaluate him for the interventional arm.

## F-019: Harvard waiting-list correction

Do not state that Lito is on a Harvard waiting list. Mila previously corrected
that description. The narrower reported understanding was that Broad/Harvard
might evaluate him when an opportunity reopened, not that he held a guaranteed
queue position. An August 24 report mentioned October 20 in that context but did
not establish a reserved place, screening appointment, eligibility, or access.

## Unresolved site activity

An August 24 report described a possible Nashville/Vanderbilt conversation and
clinician contact with Mayo involving medical records. No current outcome is
confirmed here. These leads require reconfirmation and must not be described as
screening, acceptance, or treatment.

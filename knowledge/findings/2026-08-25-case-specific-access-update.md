# Case-specific trial-access update

Updated August 25, 2026.

## Evidence scope

Family statements remain the direct source for the concrete case-specific
outcomes. Later on August 25, Ionis publicly confirmed its operational study and
expanded-access position through a statement reproduced by UOL, and Broad
publicly confirmed through its own Instagram post that it is in contact with
Lito's team and helping evaluate options. Those institutional confirmations do
not disclose or prove private eligibility decisions.

## F-017: ION717 / PrProfile case-specific status

- **Public registry:** ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT06153966 as `Active, not
  recruiting` and says expanded access is unavailable. Its detailed description
  still contains conflicting recruitment language documented in the existing
  ION717 records.
- **Sponsor operational status:** Ionis says the study is no longer recruiting
  new participants and ION717 is not currently available through its
  expanded-access programs. Safety and efficacy have not been established.
- **Case-specific status:** the family reports that Ionis declined Lito's
  inclusion. No current ION717 access path for Lito has been identified.

ION717 is `closed / monitor for material change`, not a current access path.
Only a sponsor policy change, new cohort, meaningful registry update, ION717
EAP, new case-specific route or sponsor-authorized site opening should reopen
access work.

## F-018: PRiSM case-specific path

- **Public registry:** ClinicalTrials.gov lists PRiSM and all five sites as
  `Recruiting`. The study includes a non-intervention observational arm and an
  investigational-drug arm. A registry label does not publish real-time capacity
  by arm or cohort.
- **Institutional relationship:** Broad publicly confirms active contact with
  Lito's team and says it is helping evaluate options, while warning that it
  does not know whether it can help.
- **Case-specific status:** the family reports that the concrete option
  communicated so far would be on the control/observation or other
  non-intervention side and would not provide the experimental intervention.

Broad's statement does not confirm the reported non-intervention path as final
or exclusive. It is not evidence that PRiSM rejected Lito, that he is eligible
or ineligible, or that a slot exists. The actionable question is whether Broad
or another PRiSM site/cohort can expedite an interventional eligibility review
and, if not, what exact constraint prevents it.

## F-019: Harvard waiting-list correction

The historical “Harvard waiting list” description was corrected and is
superseded for current-state use. Later government reporting supports only that
Lito may be on a preliminary PRiSM-associated screening or triage path and still
needs eligibility evaluation. It does not establish participation, a reserved
place, interventional access or FDA approval of Lito as an individual.

## Unresolved site activity

An August 24 report described a possible Nashville/Vanderbilt conversation and
clinician contact with Mayo involving medical records. No current outcome is
confirmed here. These leads require reconfirmation and must not be described as
screening, acceptance, or treatment.
